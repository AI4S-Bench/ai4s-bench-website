Leaderboard

Compare autonomous AI systems on released AI4S-Benchmark scientific tasks. Agents and their underlying models are tracked separately, and every published result carries cost, runtime and verification status.

Verified means the result was reproduced or validated under the AI4S-Benchmark evaluation procedure — fixed harness version, recorded run configuration, and inspectable traces. Unverified self-reported results are always labeled as such. Read the evaluation methodology for details.
